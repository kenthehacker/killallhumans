## Safe restart sequence

From PowerShell in the repository:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.aigp_vq2_run --stage preflight --record
```

`preflight` is passive: it does not arm or send flight targets. It requires a
fresh heartbeat, IMU, race status, actuator stream, camera stream at at least
20 decoded frames/s, a calibrated attitude estimate, and a stable gate target.

Only after preflight passes, the already-calibrated bounded sign check is:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.aigp_vq2_run --stage sign-id --record
```

Other powered stages exist for development:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.aigp_vq2_run --stage hover --record
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.aigp_vq2_run --stage gate0 --record
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.aigp_vq2_run --stage gate0-observe --record
```

Treat `hover`, `gate0`, and `gate0-observe` as explicit live-flight tests, not
routine startup checks. `gate0` is bounded to five seconds and stops as soon
as race status advances on a strictly newer packet. `gate0-observe` preserves
that same bound, sends only exact zero commands after credit, and waits at most
0.20 seconds for three distinct gate-1 frames before cleanup. Neither stage
attempts gate 1.

`--record` chooses a timestamped compressed JSONL path under `captures/`.
Captures are intentionally ignored by Git.

## Build 3385 interface findings

Unlike the older VQ1 build, VQ2 build 3385 does not provide usable
`ATTITUDE`, `LOCAL_POSITION_NED`, `ODOMETRY`, or gate-map geometry. The working
inputs are:

- `HIGHRES_IMU`
- UDP JPEG vision
- heartbeat
- race status
- actuator output status
- collision events

The runner therefore creates `AIGPMavlinkAdapter` with
`telemetry_mode="imu"` and `fetch_track_on_connect=False`. It commands only
body rates plus thrust with `SET_ATTITUDE_TARGET`'s attitude-ignore mask. The
adapter intentionally rejects quaternion-attitude commands in IMU mode.

Measured VQ2 desired-rate-to-gyro signs are `(-1, -1, -1)`. The legacy pose
mode mapping remains `(-1, +1, -1)`. Yaw control in the VQ2 runner is exactly
zero because yaw sign/authority has not been safely calibrated in flight.

The simulator repeats each UDP JPEG chunk roughly 50-60 times, producing about
55,000 datagrams/s. `competition.vq2_vision.VQ2VisionThread` suppresses chunks
by `(frame_id, chunk_id)` before decode and isolates that load from the 50 Hz
control loop. Do not replace it with synchronous polling in the runner.

- `scripts/aigp_vq2_run.py`: bounded preflight, sign-ID, hover, first-gate, and

- Corresponding tests live beside each component and in
  `tests/test_aigp_vq2_runner.py`.

The detector's metric distance and corner fields are placeholders. The runner
uses only filtered pixel geometry and never treats those fields as calibrated
3D measurements.

## Safety contract

Do not weaken these invariants while iterating:

1. A powered stage must prove **both** race-clock and IMU-clock rollback after
   `SIM_RESET`, observe multiple advancing samples, and witness countdown.
2. Stop the vision socket during reset and restart it only after the new epoch
   is proved; camera frame IDs/timestamps do not roll back in this build.
3. Wait until GO plus 150 ms before arming or sending powered commands.
4. Confirm arm/disarm only on a heartbeat newer than the arm/disarm attempt.
5. Pace commands no faster than 50 Hz, dropping missed ticks rather than
   replaying them, below the simulator's 100 Hz command limit.
6. Abort on stale/non-advancing IMU or race data, stale camera/target, estimator
   failure, unsafe collision, non-finite command, excessive angle/rate, or a
   command outside the calibrated envelope.
7. Keep commanded yaw rate at zero and clamp roll/pitch command rates to
   0.25 rad/s.
8. Cleanup must always send reset, then attempt to prove the clean epoch and
   disarmed state. A failed cleanup means the stage result is failed.
9. Once a credible close crossing loses the target, latch zero-rate/zero-thrust
   and wait at most 0.40 s for a strictly newer race packet. Vision alone never
   declares a pass.
10. `hover`/`gate0` may tolerate at most 12 tiny launch-pad contacts totaling
    0.05 impulse during the first 0.35 s; later or larger contacts abort.
11. Post-pass observation requires a proved same-epoch gate `0 -> 1` race
    transition, resets only the stale target tracker, sends only exact zero
    commands, preserves every non-target watchdog, and ends within the earliest
    of pass + 0.20 s, crossing confirmation + 0.40 s, or flight start + 5.0 s.
    Three distinct newer frames are required; observation never authorizes a
    gate-1 attempt.

Training can initially report heartbeat `base_mode=193` (armed bit set) while
actuator demand is zero. Powered stages normalize this to disarmed after their
proved reset instead of trusting the boot state.

## Live results already established

- Passive preflight: about 31 decoded frames/s with fresh required streams.
- Spawn attitude: roll near 0, pitch about `-0.3107 rad` (`-17.80 deg`).
- Initial gate: approximately bbox `(282, 134, 80, 80/81)`, center `(322, 174)`.
- Clean sign-ID: positive roll request measured `+0.080825 rad/s`; positive
  pitch request measured `+0.158619 rad/s`; maximum attitude excursion was
  `0.018711 rad` (about 1.07 deg); cleanup confirmed.
- Stronger 0.32-thrust launch stabilized roll/pitch and produced a repeatable,
  centered approach.
- Gate 0 passed collision-free. Confirmation armed at bbox
  `(75, 0, 518, 360)`, cut thrust when the full-frame target became stale, and
  accepted gate index 1 only when race time advanced from 5996 to 6246 ms.
- The bounded observation repeat also passed gate 0 collision-free, with the
  decisive race packet advancing from 6005 to 6256 ms. All three post-pass
  setpoints were exact zero commands, spaced about 31 ms apart.
- Gate 1 was already detectable before race credit, but the stale full-frame
  gate-0 tracker correctly rejected it. A tracker-only reset then acquired
  three consecutive frames in 0.093 s: bbox `(477, 4, 106, 97)`, then
  `(477, 0, 112, 99)`, then `(479, 0, 115, 97)`, with center moving from
  `(530, 52)` to `(536, 48)`.
- At acquisition the attitude was stable near roll `0.00320 rad` (`0.18 deg`)
  and pitch `-0.05094 rad` (`-2.92 deg`), with peak body rate below
  `0.002 rad/s`. The next gate was high-right and partly top-clipped.
- Cleanup logged gate index 1 before reset, then confirmed disarm and the new
  reset epoch. No collision or abort event was recorded.
- No steering toward gate 1 and no full-race attempt has occurred.

## Next engineering step

Design a bounded gate-1 recentering stage from the captured high-right target.
Do not jump directly to a full pass. First add top-clipped square-center
inference, pixel-motion damping, and a short authority-limited maneuver that
can move gate 1 toward a conservative image corridor, then stop and prove
cleanup. It must retain the same race/IMU/camera/collision watchdogs and treat
any gate-index change as an abort until a separate gate-1 crossing policy is
reviewed.

The current symmetric pixel-rate damping remains unchanged so the successful
gate-0 trajectory is reproducible. The observation shows that gate 1 is
available to the detector, while tracker state—not HSV segmentation—caused the
pre-credit rejection. A more robust long-term perception path should expose
the inner aperture and treat clipped outer-box dimensions as censored rather
than measured geometry.
